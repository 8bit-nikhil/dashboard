Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything — tasks, notes, projects, calendar events, and settings — is saved automatically in your browser's LocalStorage. No account or backend server is required.

How does the Weather tool get live forecasts?

NEXORA fetches real-time meteorological data and geocoding directly from the public Open-Meteo API.

Does the Focus Timer keep running when I switch tools?

Yes! Your Focus Timer runs seamlessly in the background across all NEXORA views, and its live countdown remains visible in the sidebar.
